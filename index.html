About Me

Entrepreneur at Heart

I'm an entrepreneur and product builder based in Madison, Wisconsin. I founded Tchola, a social financial platform now in development, and I run Market Soleil L.L.C., a small studio that has shipped four iOS apps. Before building companies in Madison, I ran my own business in Haiti. I hold degrees in International Business and Digital Marketing from the University of Wisconsin–Whitewater, and I also work as an Enrollment Coordinator at Madison College and part-time at Apple.

Building Bridges

With fluency in English, French, Haitian Creole, and Spanish, I specialize in connecting communities and creating solutions for underserved markets. My ventures focus on addressing real needs in diaspora communities while promoting diversity and inclusion.

What I'm Working Toward

  • Launching Tchola. Taking Tchola public and building it into a company meant to outlast me.
  • Making films. I'm writing my first feature film, set in Port-au-Prince, and training as an actor so I can play the lead. Along the way I'm learning AI filmmaking.
  • Publishing my first book. Changing Habits: A Practical Guide to Building a Better Life, One Habit at a Time, with a workbook edition, headed for Amazon, and a French translation after that.
  • Building in public. Sharing what I build and learn on YouTube, including a weekly series where I ship a real app with AI tools.
  • Always learning. Going deeper into how AI models work, and one day into chip design.
